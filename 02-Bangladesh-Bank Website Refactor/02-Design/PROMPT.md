# PROMPT — Design Phase: New Bangladesh Bank Website

Act as a senior UI/UX designer. We will design the new Bangladesh Bank website together.

## What I give you

1. **The live website:** https://www.bb.org.bd/en/index.php (screenshot attached: `Banglades-Bank-Home.png`)
2. **The UX audit report:** `BB_UX_Audit_Report.html` — 9 problems we found on the live site (5 High, 3 Medium, 1 Low, 3 WCAG failures).

## What I want

A new homepage design as **one HTML file** that I can open in a browser.

- Keep all the real content of the live site: market rates, news, policy rates, services, hotlines.
- Fix every problem from the audit report.
- Keep the Bangladesh Bank identity: the green and red of the national flag, official and trustworthy.
- English and Bangla, with a language switch.
- Include 4 inner pages that share one layout: Monetary Policy, Financial System, Publications, Services.

## How we work

1. First, read the report and the website. Tell me the top 5 problems in plain words.
2. Show me 2 design directions in short text. Wait for me to pick one.
3. Make the design.
4. I give feedback and you change it. We repeat until I approve.

At the end, give me a short list: each audit problem and how the new design fixes it.
