Codex vs Claude Code

Both are AI helpers that write and fix code for you. The big difference is simple: where they do the work, and how much they need you watching over them.

OpenAI

Codex

Works on its own, in the cloud

You give it a clear task and walk away. It does the work on OpenAI's computers and shows you the finished result. Like handing a job to a contractor.

In a nutshell → the do-it-for-me helper. Fast, cheaper, hands-off.
Anthropic

Claude Code

Works with you, on your computer

It works on your actual files, right there with you, step by step. You guide it as it goes. Like a colleague sitting next to you figuring things out together.

In a nutshell → the work-with-me helper. Thorough, deeper, hands-on.

# Bottom line

It really comes down to one choice: do you want a helper that runs off and does the job for you, or one that works beside you and thinks it through with you? Pick Codex when the task is clear and you just want it done fast and cheap. Pick Claude Code when the problem is messy and you want care, depth, and control. Plenty of people use both — Claude to figure things out, Codex to crank through the routine stuff.
